# Shipping

## Delivery times
Standard shipping takes 3 to 5 business days. Express shipping takes 1 business day if the order is placed before 14:00.

## Shipping costs
Standard shipping is free for orders over 50 EUR; otherwise it costs 4.99 EUR. Express shipping costs 12.99 EUR.

## International shipping
The store ships to all European Union countries. Delivery takes 5 to 8 business days and costs 14.99 EUR. The store does not ship outside the EU.

## Order statuses
"Pending" means payment is not confirmed yet. "Processing" means the warehouse is preparing the order. "Shipped" means the package left the warehouse and has a tracking number. Orders can only be cancelled while they are Pending or Processing.
