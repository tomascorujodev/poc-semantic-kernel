# Warranty

## Coverage period
All products have a 2-year manufacturer warranty from the delivery date. Laptops from the "Pro" line have an extended 3-year warranty.

## What is covered
The warranty covers hardware defects under normal use: dead pixels (more than 3), battery failure (less than 70% capacity in the first year), and faulty ports or keys.

## What is not covered
Accidental damage, liquid damage, and normal wear (scratches, worn keycaps) are not covered. Opening the device or repairing it at an unauthorized shop voids the warranty.

## How to make a claim
Customers contact support with the order id and a description or photo of the problem. The store sends a prepaid shipping label. Repairs take 10 to 15 business days; if the product cannot be repaired, it is replaced.
